Four ways this raises revenue for NIGP.

Three of the four exist today in some form on the current engagement. The fourth is the product path. None of them requires NIGP Consulting to hire a data team.

01 · Price the data deliverables
Dashboards and baseline metrics as a line item
Deliverable 2 in the current scope (performance dashboard, baseline metrics, KPI framework) is a priced, repeatable work product, not a by-product of the report. Every assessment carries it.
Per engagement
02 · Add the portal to every proposal
A deliverable competitors cannot match
The client portal becomes a standard offer in NIGP Consulting responses. It raises win rate on assessments and justifies a higher fee on the analysis phases.
Win rate + fee
03 · Keep the client after the report
Annual portal licence per client
The client's team reloads new spend extracts, new interviews and new drafts, and the portal rebuilds with history. NIGP Consulting earns a recurring licence after the engagement ends.
Recurring
04 · A lighter product for members
Maturity assessment as a stand-alone offer
The Procurement Intelligence Framework placement (six domains, five stages) can be sold on its own to NIGP member agencies as a short engagement that leads into a full assessment.

Six domains, six steps, one maturity position.

The Procurement Intelligence Framework in the current proposal is the spine of this offering. Domains are assessed in dependency order, each through the same six steps, and the result places the client on a five-stage maturity model with evidence.

Three ways to partner. One recommended.

Start with B for a fixed term, with C written in as the path once the product is ready. Terms are best agreed off the first joint proposal, not in the abstract.

Option A
Subcontract
Services bought per engagement at a fixed price per line.
NIGP gets: flexibility, no commitment.
Fits when: one more client is wanted as a test.
Option B · Recommended
Capability partner
Services plus a standing retainer: evidence custody, the AI answering service for NIGP analysts, the method library, proposal support. Exclusive to NIGP Consulting in public procurement for a fixed term.
NIGP gets: a differentiator competitors cannot buy, lower cost per engagement.
Fits when: the portal is part of how NIGP Consulting wins work.
Option C
Product channel
The portal as a co-branded NIGP product with a revenue share per client licence.
NIGP gets: recurring revenue after the engagement ends.
Fits when: two or three engagements have proved the portal.
