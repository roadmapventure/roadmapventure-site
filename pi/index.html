GovTech Data & Technology Consultant | Austin, Texas
Overview

John centers his consulting on procurement and location (GIS) data analytics, performance measurement, maturity assessment, ERP integrations and product optimization, organization and process management, and AI readiness for public-sector organizations.

RMV has built products that automate the consulting practice and provides an AI agent-as-a-Service(AaaS) platform.

Procurement Focus

He served for eight years as Director of Product and Data Practice at SOVRA, where he oversaw business requirements, product design, and release strategy for its software portfolio. That work gave him direct familiarity with both buyer and supplier perspectives — not just how procurement systems are built, but how they are used across purchasing workflows, supplier engagement, and spend reporting.

He launched SOVRA's Data Practice, defining the customer metrics and KPIs that drive contract and spend behavior, and led data practice workshops for state-level government clients on structuring and maturing their procurement data.

Early career

Earlier, as GIS Coordinator for the City of Kansas City, Missouri, he built applications integrated with the City's permitting and land-records system and coordinated data-sharing and governance policies across four counties.

Wanting to dive deeper into analytics, John spent time as a management consultant and implementing SAS BI products integrated to GIS software.

John has 4 U.S. PTO patent filings, 2 issued covering finance risk and location analytics.
